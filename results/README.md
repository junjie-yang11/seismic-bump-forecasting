# Saved evidence by study stage

| Directory | Contents | Guide |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `phase1/` | Forecast predictions, probability assessment, engineering comparisons and model explanations | [Stage 1 evidence](phase1/README.md) |
| `phase2/` | Historical reference scores, selected thresholds, transferred decisions, loss and capacity results | [Stage 2 evidence](phase2/README.md) |

The final papers are centralized under [reports/](../reports/README.md). Dated review editions and execution logs are archived under `docs/audits/`; they are separate from the current experimental evidence. Undefined quantities retain documented empty values; infinite cutoffs are decision sentinels. Hypothetical costs and prevalence scenarios are identified separately from observed monitoring labels.
